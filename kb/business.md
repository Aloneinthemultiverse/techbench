## Hours and location

Our service counter is open Monday to Friday, eight in the morning until six in the evening.
We are closed on Saturday and Sunday, and on public holidays.
Our workshop is at Unit 14, Northgate Industrial Estate.
Emergency breakdown support runs twenty four hours for customers on a service contract.

## Orders and delivery

Standard delivery takes two to three working days within the country.
Next day delivery is available if the order is placed before two in the afternoon.
Delivery is free on orders over five thousand rupees.
We do not ship outside the country at present.

## Returns and refunds

Unused parts can be returned within thirty days of delivery for a full refund.
Parts that have been fitted or modified cannot be returned.
Refunds are processed within seven working days of the part arriving back with us.
The customer pays return postage unless the part was faulty or we sent the wrong item.
A faulty part is replaced at no cost, including postage both ways.

## Warranty

All parts carry a twelve month warranty from the date of delivery.
Seals and consumables are covered for ninety days.
Warranty does not cover damage from incorrect fitting or from operating outside rated temperature.

## Payments

We accept card, bank transfer, and account terms for approved trade customers.
Trade accounts are settled thirty days from invoice.
We do not accept cash at the service counter.

## Complaints and escalation

A complaint is acknowledged within one working day and resolved within five.
If a caller is not satisfied, escalate to the service manager.
Anything involving injury, or a part failing in service, must be escalated immediately and never resolved on the call.

## What we do not answer on a call

We do not quote for bespoke fabrication over the phone.
We do not give fitting instructions for parts outside our catalogue.
We do not discuss another customer's order.
